How These Are Computed NO NETWORK CALL
Sun times come from the NOAA solar-position algorithm evaluated for the observatory's own coordinates — not from a weather API. The station therefore knows when the sun rises even with no internet at all, which matters because this drives observation scheduling. Accuracy is within a few minutes; the algorithm ignores local terrain, so a hill on the horizon will always delay real sunrise past the computed one.
