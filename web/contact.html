Data & Code BOTH PUBLIC

Observations go to the e-Callisto central archive, where the team at ETH Zurich screens, classifies and publishes them. The archive is public — this station's files sit alongside every other station's, and anyone may use them.

The monitoring software on this site is open source. If you are setting up a station and want to do something similar, it is dependency-free and meant to be read.
